1Your seal

Your seal is a key and the artwork in its center. Make one, or open a key file you saved before.

This is how your seal will look. The dots in the ring change with every document.

Save the key file and keep the password. With both you can seal on any device. This page keeps the key file on this device, encrypted with your password.

2Your document

Any file: a PDF, a picture, a spreadsheet. You get back a PDF with the seal on its first page and your file attached unchanged.

    Download the sealed PDF

    To check it: open the scanner, scan the seal, then tap “Check against a document…” and pick this PDF.

    3Finish the time proof

    A few hours after sealing, pick the sealed PDF. The page fetches the Bitcoin part of the proof and gives you the finished PDF.
